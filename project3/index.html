Frequently Asked Questions

Every garment in our catalog is engineered with custom mill-woven fabrics, including 500+ GSM loopback French Terry, water-impermeable 3-layer GORE-TEX membranes, and industrial YKK hardware. Our cuts prioritize modern oversized architecture with drop shoulders and articulated joints.
Our apparel is intentionally cut with a streetwear-forward, boxy, relaxed fit. If you prefer the intended oversized streetwear drape, select your true standard size. If you prefer a tailored or slimmer fit, we suggest ordering one size down. Footwear fits true to standard Nike sizing.
We offer Free Express Worldwide Shipping on all orders over $150 (delivered in 2–4 business days via DHL Express). Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery in unworn condition with original security tags intact.
Wash inside-out with cold water on a gentle cycle. Hang dry or lay flat out of direct sunlight to preserve cotton density and vintage pigment dyes. For technical outerwear shells, wipe down with a damp cloth or use dedicated technical garment wash to preserve DWR coatings.
